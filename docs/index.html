The problem

Your AI configs are everywhere

Every AI coding tool stores settings in its own scattered JSON, TOML, and markdown files across your home directory and project folders. Finding what's configured where is a scavenger hunt nobody signed up for.

~/.claude/settings.json .gemini/settings.json ~/.codex/config.yaml .cursor/mcp.json .claude/commands/ ~/.gemini/gemini_agents/ AGENTS.md .github/copilot/

What it does

Everything in one place

Loadout scans, organizes, and syncs your AI tool configs so you don't have to.
